No be to start lifestyle, you go fit maintain am?

Published on Date • By Nicolletta Eneji • 5 min read

You see those TikTok posts that leave you shouting, “God, when?”

The ones that make you drop comments like, “God, if I’m a tree, send me to the evil forest.”

When you finally taste the life and it spoils, you go cry wetin no good.

Every babe wants to have a rich boyfriend, a rich and generous one. Yes, including me.

We all want to send snaps of the red roses he got us, the Ferrero Rocher, the Coach Tabby 20s, and many other goodies. We want to be the benchmark for people’s “God, when?” moments during the biannual Hallelujah Challenge. But have we stopped to think about what happens to that lifestyle when the relationship ends?

Let me tell you what hits you when you’ve made yourself at home on a man’s card, with no job – or a job too weak to fund the lifestyle you’ve embraced.

Taste pizza once, and the garri you once loved will taste like poison. I wish someone had advised Amaka before she started flying too close to the sun.

Maintenance that she used to manage once in two months, she started doing biweekly.

Restaurants she used to visit twice a year—her birthday and New Year’s Eve—she started visiting three times a week. She liked how “crème brûlée” rolled off her tongue as she read it from the menu to the waiter dressed in a black tuxedo.

Amaka, you that used to buy jollof rice without chicken from Calabar Pot are now paying ₦55,000 for steak and grass served on an oversized plate. You said you were paying for the experience. Experience that will end in the toilet. Oya now.

Amaka, you said I was classless because I hadn’t gone to Rararamen. I told you I couldn’t pay that amount just to cook Indomie for myself when there’s a gas cooker at home. You said it was for the thrill, and that I shouldn’t leave this earth without tasting the good things.

You bought the same blouse in five different colours from Mapletoby. I told you to come with me to Karimo market so you’d stop wasting money. You said you didn’t like the mud that came with the season. Ha! Amaka. They gave birth to you during Benin’s rainy season, and you’re now allergic to mud? Okay.

As if all this was not enough, you openly brought out two ₦1,000 notes from your purse to give me when you saw that I was about to drop ₦50 in the offering basket on Easter Sunday. Amaka, eight people behind us saw the exact amount. Me that squeezed my ₦50 in my fist—do you think I’m stupid? Did your Bible not say your left hand should not know what your right hand is giving? Because everybody knew!

Amaka, your boyfriend told you to cut me off. He said that you needed to roll with babes in your class, “soft life babes”. He said you should be friends with his friends’ girlfriends because they were on your level. And you actually cut me off because of that Pablo.

Now my heart is full of joy, and I don’t care if people call me a witch. Last week, I saw you buying ugwu leaf in Dutse market. The moment I heard you say “ugwu” instead of the spinach you had upgraded to, I knew something was wrong. Hearing you bargain for ₦1,000 worth of ugwu at ₦700 confirmed it.

Ladies and gentlemen, my friend’s odogwu broke up with her.

The silly girl didn’t even bother to look for a job while she was in the relationship—she thought she was a permanent resident. I don’t know what made her think someone with an SS genotype would marry her, an AS babe. The math was never mathing!

Even if Amaka decided to get a job today, which one will sponsor ₦35,000 hybrid lashes twice a month, frequent hair installations, coffin nails, and meals “for the experience”? How was she going to pay her house rent in Jabi after saying Dutse Alhaji wasn’t her standard? How was Amaka going to attend Jaybash’s drift show with her “soft life” friends when she no longer had Odogwu’s card?

Now her vocabulary has reversed. She’s calling spinach ugwu again. See ehn, if you don’t have the kind of money that you want a man to spend on you, it’s better you don’t taste it in the first place. Human beings never want to go back to garri after tasting pizza. Garri will give you depression o.

Please don’t be like my friend Amaka. Post-relationship clarity is not funny. Anyway, she apologized yesterday. We’re going shopping at Karimo this weekend to rebuild our relationship.

A word is enough for the wise.
